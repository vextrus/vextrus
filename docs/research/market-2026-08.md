# The market, re-examined — 16 August 2026

Four independent investigations against primary sources (vendor docs, release notes, standards
texts, government PDFs, press releases) plus one adversarial verification of two Bangladesh
pricing-rule claims the domain law carried. Secondary sources are `[S]`; retrieved 2026-08-16
unless the source states its own date.

## 1. Revision-stable quantity identity — still unoccupied

**RIB CostX** is unchanged since the first founding's correction. Current build is CostX 7.4
Revision 1 (03-08-2026); no 7.5/8.0 exists. The 7.4 release notes contain no revision-matching
change (grep for revis/compar/best match/delta/object id: no hits)
(https://techweb.exactal.com/downloads/upgrade.php;
https://www.rib-software.com/pdf/en/rib-costx-7.4-release-notes.pdf). The mechanism is still
vector-line matching: Overlay (visual, manual realign), Compare by Lines, and Compare by Object,
which "recognises unique object IDs" — supplied by the CAD/BIM file. The offset concession is
still live documentation: if "the drawing has been offset by the designer… no lines will match"
(https://www.rib-software.com/en/blogs/rib-costx-drawing-comparison-tool, 26 Jan 2017;
Auto-Revisioning at https://www.rib-software.com/en/blogs/rib-costx-auto-drawing-revisioning,
8 Mar 2019). RIB's marketing claim is deliberately soft — "Update takeoff and estimates based on
revised designs while maintaining an audit trail." Verdict per failure mode: re-origin/offset
fails (documented); re-authoring fails (segments differ); raster re-issue fails (no primitives,
no IDs) — only visual Overlay remains.

**Bluebeam** Batch Slip Sheet copies markups (measurements are markups) to the revised page at
the same **page coordinates**, with no warning and no mechanism for content that moved
(https://support.bluebeam.com/user-manual/menus/batch/slip-sheet.html). Bluebeam Max (19 May
2026) adds Smart Overlay — matches sheets by title block, "doesn't currently include an
auto-alignment feature… so these differences appear as changes", rasterized output, still
Preview, no quantity involvement (https://support.bluebeam.com/revu/resources/smart-overlay-review-faq.html).
Revu 21.10 (2 Jun 2026): nothing on revision transfer.

**Autodesk Forma Takeoff** (renamed 2026) forces a lossy choice on a new sheet version: "move
the takeoff directly to the new version, or discard all existing takeoff" — a relocation, no
geometry re-validation; sheet identity is by sheet number
(https://help.autodesk.com/cloudhelp/ENU/Takeoff-Files/files/Updating_Outdated.html). Object-ID
added/removed/changed diffs exist only for RVT models via API. Jan 2026 release notes: nothing
on revisions. Customer idea "Keep takeoff on previous versions" (3 Sep 2025) still gathering
support `[S]` (https://forums.autodesk.com/t5/forma-for-construction-ideas/keep-takeoff-on-previous-versions/idi-p/13794908).

**Countfire** is the closest shipping reuse: "reuse existing work to automatically count
revised drawings" — with the constraint "the size & orientation of the PDF needs to be the same
for your work to correctly line up", and symbol re-matching requires the same element count and
scale (https://help.countfire.com/en/articles/64566-revised-drawings, 27 Jun 2025). Symbol-
definition matching plus page-coordinate preservation; fails on offset, re-authoring, raster.

**Togal.AI** "quantify changes with a single click" is, per its own how-to, a visual overlay
with manual markup (https://www.togal.ai/blog/how-to-compare-drawings-in-togal-ai). **Kreo**
Compare Drawings: manual two-point registration, nothing on transferring measurements
(https://help-takeoff.kreo.net/en/articles/8986211-compare-drawings, 13 Aug 2026). **STACK**:
"overlay is for visual comparison only and does not affect measurements". **Procore**: overlay
"so you can update your construction takeoff instantly" — no claim measurements carry
(https://www.procore.com/fc/takeoff). **Trimble** (30 Jun 2026), **On-Screen Takeoff** ("Do not
perform takeoff on an overlay"), **eTakeoff** 8.2-15, **Beam AI**, **Handoff**, **PlanSwift**
Takeoff Boost (6 May 2026): no revision-transfer claim.

**2026 entrants**: no launch whose wedge is revision-aware takeoff; an August 2026 roundup names
takeoffs going "stale on addendum three" as unsolved `[S]`
(https://www.deadfront.ai/blog/august-2026-ai-construction-roundup). Domain-derived identity
appears nowhere below sheet level; below the sheet every product uses coordinates, vector
geometry, symbol fingerprints or vendor object IDs.

**Verdict.** Domain-derived, coordinate-free identity that carries a quantity across a
re-origined, re-authored or scanned re-issue is unoccupied as of 16 Aug 2026. Closest: Countfire
and CostX, both conceding in their own docs the exact case that matters. Every mechanism in the
market degrades to a human-eye overlay precisely when the drawing changes most.

## 2. Trust as the product — basis and coverage unoccupied; refusal marginally occupied

**(a) Basis taxonomy.** No vendor labels a quantity by how it was derived. Closest: Beam AI
"Plan Discrepancy Callouts" shows the AI-measured value beside a value written on the plan and
lets the plan value take precedence — a two-way measured/transcribed distinction framed as a
discrepancy display, and it "does not prevent bid submission or refuse to calculate"
(https://www.ibeam.ai/features/plan-discrepancy-callouts). Autodesk "AI Transparency Cards" are
feature-level, not per-output. RIB CostX ships traceability (what changed, where a number lives),
never derivation. Ediphi↔Togal integration (4 May 2026) passes quantities and an edit history;
no basis/coverage metadata crosses (https://www.ediphi.com/blog/ediphi-and-togal-ai-announce-native-integration).
Bluebeam's own guidance makes basis a human discipline: "trace each quantity back to the mark
that produced it" (https://blog.bluebeam.com/digital-takeoff-steps/, 16 Jul 2026).

**(b) Coverage declaration.** No vendor states unmeasured scope with a named cause per omission.
Adjacent: Buildxact "Blu: Estimate Reviewer" flags "what you might have missed" against templates
(https://help.buildxact.com/en/articles/11324156-what-is-blu); Bluebeam Smart Review flags
document-consistency gaps (missing schedules, unmatched tags; Plumbing Check 7 Jul 2026) and
assigns visual coverage checking to the user ("see what got counted and — the part that matters —
what didn't"). Provision Scope Agent traces every scope item to a source page, "95% verified
accuracy", no unmeasured-scope statement (https://provision.com/announcements/provision-launches-scope-agent).
The demand is codified and unimplemented: AACE RP 34R-05 requires a Basis of Estimate with
assumptions and exclusions (https://web.aacei.org/docs/default-source/toc/toc_34r-05.pdf); RICS
now mandates a written reliability decision listing assumptions and named concerns (below).

**(c) Refusal with a named reason.** One narrow occupation: STACK Roof AI emits a measurement
only above a 60% confidence threshold, with named causes (obscured roof, low-quality image) —
aerial roof only, threshold-gated, primary help doc unreachable (403/404)
(https://www.stackct.com/blog/roof-ai-for-aerial-imagery/). Everywhere else the opposite ships:
Trimble auto-detects and sets scale with no failure state ("Estimators use their own built-in
QA/QC process to identify false positives", 30 Jun 2026); PlanSwift Auto Scale likewise;
Autodesk symbol detection pushes verification to the user. Kreo names the gap without filling
it: "Bad scale is not a UX bug. It is a wrong bid with confidence" and "Do not buy a confidence
score. Buy a verification workflow." (https://www.kreo.net/news-2d-takeoff/what-is-ai-takeoff,
3 Aug 2026).

**Confidence ≠ basis.** No vendor publishes per-line confidence in primary docs; "most platforms
give a confidence score" appears only in third-party listicles `[S]`. A confidence is a model's
self-report; a basis is a checkable claim about recourse. Independent benchmarks make the split
load-bearing: AECV-Bench (arXiv:2601.04819, 8 Jan 2026) — OCR/text tasks up to 0.95, symbol
counting of doors/windows 0.40–0.55; DrawingVQA (arXiv:2607.15418, 16 Jul 2026) on real IFC
structural drawings — best model 41.7% on quantity takeoff, several near 0%. Vendor claims live
today without substantiation: Togal "98%" (unnamed University of Kansas study, no sample or
tolerance), Beam "99%+" (human-QA'd), Kreo "94.5–98.5%", PlanSwift "97.3%", Handoff H1 "81.6%"
on a vendor-run benchmark described as 15 sets in the press release and 10 on the blog (21 Jul
2026). FTC v. Workado (final order Aug 2025) barred a "98% accurate" AI claim that measured 53%
in use — accuracy claims need competent and reliable evidence
(https://www.ftc.gov/news-events/news/press-releases/2025/08/ftc-approves-final-order-against-workado-llc-which-misrepresented-accuracy-its-artificial).

**RICS Professional Standard — verified from the text.** *Responsible use of artificial
intelligence in surveying practice*, 1st ed., September 2025, effective 9 March 2026, mandatory
("must") for AI with a material impact on a surveying service
(https://www.rics.org/content/dam/ricsglobal/documents/standards/Responsible-use-of-artificial-intelligence-in-surveying-practice_September-2025.pdf).
Verbatim: a written reliability decision "must be prepared by, or under the supervision of, an
appropriately qualified and **named** surveyor who accepts responsibility"; it must detail
assumptions, key areas of concern, the reason for each, mitigation, and a concluding statement.
§4.2: firms "**must therefore undertake randomised dip samples** of the outputs at regular
intervals" (also §3.2). Where an output cannot reasonably be used, they "must communicate such a
conclusion in writing to clients, together with their written reasoning". Written client
disclosure of AI use in advance; PI-cover extent for AI use in the terms of engagement. The
standard is discipline-agnostic (no mention of takeoff, NRM, ICMS). No enforcement case yet; no
AIQS/CIQS/ASPE/IEB equivalent found. No lawsuit or regulator action over AI takeoff quantities located.

**Verdict.** (a) unoccupied; (b) unoccupied; (c) marginally occupied in one aerial-roof corner.
The demand is now written into a mandatory professional standard (named surveyor, dip sample,
written refusal) and into independent evidence that transcription and geometry differ by 40+
points of accuracy — and no product surfaces the difference.

## 3. SoR-native cost intelligence — unoccupied, with two corrections to what we carried

**The Bangladesh books.** PWD SoR 2022 (2nd Revised), 16th edition, Part A Civil Works, effective
22 Jan 2026 (MoF memo 08/01/2026; MoHPW memo 22/01/2026), 354 pp, PDF only; embedded VAT
7.5%→10% per NBR SRO 182-Law/2025/310-VAT of 27/05/2025
(https://ss.pwd.gov.bd/document/sor/Final_SoR_2022%20_2nd_Revised.pdf). PWD's distribution page
lists nine documents, all PDF, no search, no data (https://ss.pwd.gov.bd/sor). PWD Design Unit:
estimating software "under preparation" (undated) (http://m.pwd.gov.bd/about/design_units).
LGED SoR-2022 3rd ed., Aug 2025, zone-banded A–D (A Dhaka & Mymensingh · B Chattogram & Sylhet ·
C Rajshahi & Rangpur · D Khulna & Barishal), PDF only
(https://file-dhaka.portal.gov.bd/uploads/51301c6d-c11e-4918-aacd-4b2f108f873e//696/537/c58/696537c581a7b310285230.pdf).
RHD SoR: rhd.gov.bd redirects to a host with an untrusted certificate; effectively unreachable.

**Checked claim — PWD zoning.** A first pass grepped the 2nd Revised PDF for "Zone" and found
nothing; the adversarial re-read (§5) found the four columns on every rate page, labelled by
district group. `bd-authority.md` §4 stands, with one precision: the letters A–D appear only in
the original 2022 edition; 2023 and 2026 label the columns by district names alone.

**Nobody structures them.** Google Play "PWD rate schedule": no BD or Indian SoR app. GitHub:
zero repositories for `"schedule of rates" bangladesh`, `"CPWD" DSR estimate`, `PWD BOQ
estimate bangladesh`. The market's "SoR software" is individual Excel workbooks on YouTube (May
2026, Mar 2026). SoR commerce is print/PDF retail (Daraz, procurementbd.com). TenderPulse
(Dhaka) reads an e-GP tender pack and extracts BOQ quantities and units — no pricing, no rate
database (https://tenderpulse.com.bd/en/ppr-explained). dorpatra.com and BDTender aggregate
notices and PDF links.

**The unified "Bangladesh Schedule of Rates".** ECNEC decision 13 May 2026; working committee
first met 22 Jun 2026 (Planning Commission, Physical Infrastructure Division) and "agreed to
examine the feasibility of a web-based platform to prepare, update and manage rate schedules"
(TBS, 5 Jul 2026, https://www.tbsnews.net/bangladesh/infrastructure/rate-schedules-govt-works-be-unified-market-based-1479721);
finalisation targeted 10 Aug 2026 then ECNEC, "location-specific adjustments" and more frequent
updates named (FE, 1 Aug 2026, https://thefinancialexpress.com.bd/trade/market-oriented-rate-schedule-for-public-projects-soon).
As of 16 Aug 2026: nothing published, no platform tender, no vendor. A window, not a competitor.

**Checked claim — the e-GP price gate.** A first pass read press coverage of the PPR 2025 draft
("removal of the ±10% limit", TBS 10 Sep 2025,
https://www.tbsnews.net/economy/govt-unveils-draft-ppr-2025-removing-local-procurement-price-cap-1233466)
as the end of the +10% ceiling. The gazetted text says otherwise (§5): the +10% ceiling over
the official estimate survives as a non-responsiveness rule; what changed is the low side, now a
weighted formula that includes e-GP market history. `bd-authority.md` §7 stands and gains the
formula. e-GP itself remains submission-only for bidders.

**India — the closest anyone has come, split in two.** Arched (arched.ai): state SoRs for 15
states/UTs as searchable tables with Excel/CSV, editions to 2026-27, an engine that "reads the
tender, maps it to the applicable schedule" — no rate analysis, no API, no correction-slip
automation, no explicit effective-dating (https://arched.ai/resources/schedule-of-rates-by-state-india,
20 Jun 2026). Costimator (costimator.in): CPWD-native cloud estimating with rate analysis and
CPWD-format tender justification, DSR 2023 in both GST methods, corrections "within days",
₹24,999–49,999/yr — one schedule (https://costimator.in/departments/cpwd). NSR-Civil: free
searchable DSR 2018/2021/2023 with correction slips and indices. CPWD itself has shipped nothing
digital; DSR corrections to April 2026 are PDF. Powerplay/Zepth/BuildSupply/Nirmaan: no
SoR-native offering surfaced.

**Region.** Nepal: Sajilo Estimate browses province→district→fiscal-year rates, no BOQ engine
(https://sajiloestimate.com/district-rates/). Pakistan MRS: bi-annual per province, no MRS-native
software. Sri Lanka BSR: annual per district, PDF. Middle East: contractor-built libraries in
Candy/CostX/Cubit; no government price book. **Global:** ACCA
PriMus still distributes all 20 Italian regional price books free in DCF, year-versioned, 2026
editions live (Basilicata OO.PP. 2026 posted 28 Jul 2026,
https://biblus.acca.it/notizie/prezzario-basilicata-oo-pp-2026-articoli-disponibile-in-dcf-per-primus/).
RIB CostX rate libraries are user-built; Glodon Cubicost markets localised measurement rules,
no regional price library; no AI takeoff vendor has announced government price-book integration.

**Verdict.** A structured, effective-dated, edition-versioned Bangladesh SoR with rate analysis
underneath is unoccupied; the government has named the gap in print and not filled it. The
nearest full stack anywhere in South Asia is two Indian products holding half each. Both carried
Bangladesh facts survived an adversarial check (§5) with added precision.

0.95 and geometry at 0.4–0.5 against a professional's 0.95, so signed quantities come from
verification and audit around the model, never from the model — which is ADR-0006.

## 5. Two domain-law claims, adversarially verified

Both claims were first *contradicted* by a quick pass and then *confirmed* by reading the
primary document itself. The lesson for future research: a keyword grep of a PDF and a press
summary of a draft are not a read of the table or the gazette.

**PWD SoR zone columns — TRUE.** Every rate table in PWD SoR 2022 (2nd Revised, eff. 22 Jan
2026; text PDF, 336 pp) has the header `Item No. | Description | Unit | Unit Rate (Tk.) →
[Dhaka, Mymensingh] [Chattogram, Sylhet] [Khulna, Barisal, Gopalgonj] [Rajshahi, Rangpur]` —
e.g. Ch. 07 RCC item 07.9 `cum 15703.00 | 15864.00 | 15025.00 | 14670.00`, book p. 109; Ch. 04
item 04.1 `cum 9,394 | 9,366 | 8,987 | 8,987` — on all 319 rate pages
(https://ss.pwd.gov.bd/document/sor/Final_SoR_2022%20_2nd_Revised.pdf). The 2022 Revised (eff.
23 Feb 2023, https://ss.pwd.gov.bd/document/sor/PWDSoR2022-Revised-2.3.23-Website.pdf) is
identical in shape. Only the original 2022 edition prints the letters (`Zone-A (Dhaka,
Mymensingh)` … `Zone-D (Rajshahi, Rangpur)`, scanned/OCR'd,
https://ss.pwd.gov.bd/document/sor/Rates%20of%20Man,%20Materials%20and%20Mark-ups.pdf). No front
matter assigns the 64 districts to a column — the headers name nine; a district→column map is
per-book config with no published authority. Ch. 33 inaccessible-area categories A/B/C
(5/10/15%) are a separate lettering. LGED's C/D are PWD's D/C. The 2018 edition could not be
reached (host no longer serves it).

**e-GP price gate — TRUE, refined.** PPR 2025 (SRO 388-Law/2025, Bangladesh Gazette
extraordinary 28 Sep 2025, 271 pp,
https://www.dpp.gov.bd/bgpress/bangla/index.php/document/get_extraordinary/58782), Schedule 18
under Rule 118(25)–(26), ¶7 (gazette p. ৯৮১৩): a tender more than 10% above the official
estimate "is not to be considered" and is non-responsive at preliminary evaluation — the worked
example (p. ৯৮১৪) rejects a bid at +12.2% and passes one at +2.86%. The low side is the
significantly-low formula `x̄ = 0.5·mean(bids) + 0.2·OCE + 0.3·NPPI` (NPPI = e-GP national
price index over a 28-day window), floor `x̄ − Sd`. Scope (¶9): domestic procurement; not
international, not outsourced physical services (classic abnormally-low test applies). LTM keeps
±5% (e-PW2B ITT 23.2/23.3/39.4); a single-tender rule at 20% (¶8). BPPA's Oct/Nov 2025 works
STDs (e-PW2A/e-PW3) do not print the +10% ceiling — rules and tender documents are out of step,
an inference from comparing the two.
