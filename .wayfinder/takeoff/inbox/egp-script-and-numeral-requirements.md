# What script does a submitted document have to be in?

## Objective

Ticket 10 ruled **Western numerals everywhere, in both languages** (grouping stays lakh/crore),
on the reasoning that a figure rendering differently per reader's locale is a second number on a
document read by two parties, and that the boundary between "prose count" and "figure" cannot be
stated well enough to enforce.

That ruling was made against a repo that **says nothing about script on a document**:
`bd-authority.md` has no language clause, `quantity-contract.md` §6 governs channels and not
script, and `formulas.md` §6 rules only that reason codes are stable identifiers which translate.
So the ruling is defensible but **unchecked against statute** — and if statute mandates a script
on a submission, statute outranks it.

Minted by ticket 10's resolving session, at the dispatcher's instruction, so the exporter is not
built on an assumption nobody tested.

## The question

1. Does **e-GP** (or the PPA/PPR under it) mandate a language or a numeral script on a submitted
   BoQ, priced schedule, or measurement document? Cite the rule, not a forum post.
2. Is there a **statutory or customary requirement** for Bangla on a tender document that a
   foreign-owned or joint-venture bidder must satisfy — and does it reach the *figures* or only
   the prose?
3. Do the **standard tender document (STD) templates** issued for works contracts render
   quantities in Bengali or Western numerals? The templates are the strongest available evidence
   of practice even where no rule exists.
4. What does **`৳` versus `Tk.` versus `BDT`** practice look like on a signed BoQ? Ticket 10 used
   `৳` throughout without ruling it, and it is the same class of question.

## What would change the ruling

A rule or template that puts **Bengali numerals in the quantity or amount column** of a submitted
document. Anything weaker — prose convention, a preference, a bilingual template that leaves
figures Western — leaves ticket 10's ruling standing and this ticket closes by confirming it.

## Guardrails

- Primary sources: the PPR/PPA text, e-GP's own published templates, CPTU. A blog is a pointer to
  a source, never the source.
- Findings land in `docs/research/egp-document-script.md` per the research ticket type.
- **This ticket does not re-open the numeral ruling** — it establishes the fact that ruling was
  made without. If the fact contradicts it, that is a new decision for a human, recorded against
  ticket 10's resolution.
