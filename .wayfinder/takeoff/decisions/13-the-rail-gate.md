# The rail gate — what a rail owes the spine

[Ticket](../tickets/13-the-rail-gate.md) · ruled 2026-08-15

A rail is a pure function returning **offers**, and the gate is the spine's **sole writer** of
quantity lines — a rail that produces *lines* authored the values the gate reads, so every check
becomes advisory. Done is the gate's return type in **three tiers**: unrepresentable
(`PARTIAL_UNDECLARED`, null calibration, prose provenance), gate-derived (both roll-ups, coverage,
attribution, the selected algebra, the refusal cause), and **nine checks**, closed and total —
§3's seven plus 03's corroboration gate, plus undeclared-attribute-role and `DEFAULTED`-on-
determining. The gate **evaluates the number** from the offer's inputs and stated expression in
**BigInt** scaled to the kind's precision, no new dependency: the rail owns geometry, the spine
owns arithmetic, and §2's variables clause stops being decorative.

The scope register is a **query, not a table** — `NOT_ESTABLISHED` is the fall-through arm with no
writer, so a residue cannot be forged and *a contradicted act suspends* is derivable rather than a
column someone must remember. Three originator classes get three mechanisms and a rail reaches
none: the act seam (`withAct`, the only construct in the repo that refuses a non-human), the
fall-through, and ingestion's verbatim counters. A rail contributes absence by **not offering**,
carrying a rail-vocabulary reason on the queue item, never a `refusalCause`. The one-way valve is
the **output type's shape** — no `absent()` constructor exists anywhere — which binds ticket 23
to the invariant while leaving it the lane mechanism.

The kind axis rides **four code-owned artifacts** — `quantityKinds`, kind→discipline,
kind→algebra (independent, because one architectural sheet bears two algebras), and `bears` on
`(class × kind)` — none stored on the register row, so nothing mutable enters the identity key.
`bears` **adds** the missing-rule instrument beside the catalogue's missing-sheet one; it never
narrows, so 01's no-actor objection does not reach it. §4 and §6 never actually contradict:
the discriminator is **what the defect impugns** — rate selection publishes unpriced, the number
keeps the row empty, admissibility hard-blocks, scope existence hard-blocks and severs. A hard
block is not silence because the query discloses it, and severance leaves nothing to subtract.
Check #9 narrowed from magnitude to **scope attribution** — the gate has neither §5's band nor
§8's sample.

The guardrail is made falsifiable by shipping a **synthetic face-shaped rail** asserting six
member-shaped assumptions the gate may not make — declared in the ruling as a **floor, not a
proof**. Rejected: a `validateLine` helper (rail-authored done in a spine-shaped hat), trusting
the rail's number (Part B is a sample, wrong instrument for a defect you can make
unrepresentable), a materialised `scope_rows` table (a filter with no actor), and landing the gate
member-shaped to lift later (the accidental refactor, with no failing state). Amends
`measurement-rules.md` §8 and `quantity-contract.md` §2/§4. Graduates two fog entries; owes two
ADR-0002 migrations and six corpus cases once 09's index exists.
