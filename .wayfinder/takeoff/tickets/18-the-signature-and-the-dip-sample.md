# The signature and the dip sample

## Objective

The destination's bill is **signed**. `quantity-contract.md` §7 and §8 specify the gates and the
sample in more detail than anything else in the domain law, and none of it exists. This ticket
rules the mechanism.

## What the domain law binds

- **The hard gate is signature, not disposition** — universal per-row confirmation degenerates
  into `confirm-all` at volume, and the money is in *absence*, which disposing rows never meets.
- **Two gates, deliberately separate**: coverage/boundary (founded outside the register, on the
  catalogue + scope register, **enumerated in full** — no sampling rate finds the row that is not
  there) and reliability (the named surveyor's written decision).
- **Generation refuses on *unsigned*, never on *bad* and never on *unknown***; adverse verdicts
  and unquantified unknowns print on the face of the bill.
- **The signed object is the boundary plus the rule set in force, not the values.** An authored
  rule re-derives and **voids every signature it moves**; a signed bill is **superseded**, never
  silently invalidated.
- **Dip sample, §8**: two instruments — quantities **sampled**, boundary **enumerated**. Part A
  is a directed review queue checked in full and never reported as a sample rate; Part B is a
  blind sample with draw unit `(register object × quantity kind)`, stratum `(class × kind)`,
  minimum one draw per quantity-bearing cell, remainder by money, level picked uniformly before
  object, and size **determined not chosen**: `N = quantity-bearing cells × 2`. Global bound:
  Part A ≤ Part B.
- **The act is blind re-derivation** — the QS enters their own figure *before* seeing the
  machine's. A shown formula that omits crank bars gives nothing to disagree with; **blind is the
  only version that catches an absence.**
- A failure **condemns the error's reach, not the draw's cell**; under → fix / move the boundary
  / qualify; **over → hard block, no qualification door.**
- Signature binds **one-to-one to its sample**; voids on boundary change, rule-set change, or a
  new revision of any cited drawing. Draws are **irrevocable**; abandonment is recorded; the
  certificate prints the failure count. **No numeric void threshold** — that judgement already
  has a name (`NOT_RELIABLE`).

## The decision

1. **`N = quantity-bearing cells × 2` against the non-functional bar.** On a 50-sheet set this
   could be a large number of blind re-derivations. Is the formula workable at real scale, or
   does it need a stated bound — and if bounded, what does the certificate say?
2. **Blind entry, as a surface.** How does a QS enter a figure without seeing ours, in a UI that
   is otherwise showing them ours everywhere?
3. **The named surveyor.** RICS *Responsible use of artificial intelligence in surveying
   practice*, 1st ed., published 17 Nov 2025, **in force 9 March 2026** — verified. It requires a
   written reliability decision by a **named** surveyor, AI in Terms of Engagement, risk
   registers, and **procurement due diligence**. Note: **"dip samples on automated output" was
   NOT verifiable** from rics.org and the repo's claim should be softened where it appears until
   the full standard is read.
4. **Money-weighted allocation with no rates.** §8 allocates the sample remainder **by money**,
   and this destination has no prices. Rule the substitute, or rule that allocation degrades to
   count and say so.

## Guardrails

- Signing is its own permission; credential captured write-once; structured verdict
  (`RELIABLE | RELIABLE_WITH_QUALIFICATIONS | NOT_RELIABLE`) with mandatory prose
  (`identity.md` §8).
- **Absence draws are a census, not a sample**, over human-authored declarations only — *name the
  evidence or lose the cause*. A withdrawal **voids the bill**: a wrong boundary is not deferrable.
- The RICS **procurement due-diligence** clause obliges regulated firms to demand artefacts
  nobody ships. That is a commercial opportunity; note it, do not build to it here.
