## Objective

`takeoff-core` 07 ruled that vertical classes take the lawful-null `UNRESOLVED` level basis
because **authoring a level is a human act** and no surface exists. 08 then recorded that the
`@unregistered:<label>` → `<levelId>` one-hop carry is **unbuilt**, having no filed dispositions
to move. The first vertical slice measures **RCC column concrete**, and `formulas.md` §2 says
**vertical members expand per level from the level stack**. Without levels there is no column
volume. This is on the critical path.

## The decision

1. **Authoring.** A level is project-scoped with a **surrogate id**; label, ordinal and height
   are **all non-identifying** (`identity.md` §2 — a QS may rename `MEZZ`, and a rename that
   re-keys the ledger orphans every link on that level). Rule the authoring act and its shape.
2. **The one-hop carry, built.** `identity.md` §3: authoring a level moves an instance key
   **exactly once**; on rebuild a miss looks one hop back so filed human dispositions move with
   the key. **Exactly one hop exists.** Rule how "exactly one" is enforced rather than intended.
3. **Storey height's basis.** `measurement-rules.md` §7: `TRANSCRIBED` / `DERIVED` / `ENTERED`
   are lawful; **`DEFAULTED` is barred** because height is quantity-determining. An `ENTERED`
   height is **declared and dip-sampled, not blocked** — no gate can detect present-and-wrong.
4. **The ordinal is physical**, and the floor-multiplier scheme keys to it. §7 records the
   failure this prevents: label-joined multipliers priced an eighth-floor item at ground rate
   (`F1` vs `1F`) — the wrong-rate class **no quantity tolerance catches**. A scheme with no row
   for an ordinal **throws**. (Multipliers are pricing and out of scope, but the ordinal
   contract is not — get it right now.)
5. **The vertical coverage denominator.** §7: the level stack **is** it, and the certificate
   prints the stack's own basis. Rule how a stack that is itself incomplete is declared.
6. **Banded verticals.** `formulas.md` §2: a banded vertical prices each band's own section ×
   its own count, and **a level no band covers defers — never inherits an adjacent band's
   section.**

## Guardrails

- Per-floor rollups sort **by ordinal, never lexicographic** (§7).
- Foundation classes take the `FOUNDATION` lawful-null basis; level-null rows are **scoped per
  view — no silent cross-view union** (`identity.md` §3).
- An act row and its state change **commit in one transaction or neither**, enforced at the seam.
